Language Learning

My Language Learning Journey

I have always been fascinated by languages and the way they connect people from different cultures.

Korean: A2-B1

Korean is a beautiful language with a rich cultural history. I am currently studying the basics of Korean grammar and vocabulary. I started in 2021 but was not consistent with my studies. I listen to K-pop to immerse myself in the language. My goal is to reach a conversational level to be able to have conversations in Korean confidently. I have started to become more consistent. I am trying new methods of learning to see what sticks. I am looking to get a tutor as well.

Japanese: A1-A2

Japanese is the second language I want to learn. It is more difficult than Korean, but I like the sound of the language. The strokes, learning Hiragana and Katakana are not as easy as I thought. With Japanese, it does not have a lof of nuance grammar like Korean. So that part is easier. But the strokes are challenging.

Spanish: A1-A2

I started learning Spanish in high school but decided to focus on other languages. I recently just started learning Spanish again. Its kind of easy picking up the alphabet and some other grammer rules. Some of the vocabulary is easy as well. The most challenging part is the articles.

Italian: A1-A2

I am currently learning Italian and finding it quite enjoyable. The pronunciation is unique and the grammar has some interesting aspects. I am focusing on building my vocabulary and improving my listening skills.

Chinese: A1-A2

Learning Chinese is a significant challenge for me. The writing system is completely different from what I'm used to. I am working on mastering the basic characters and improving my understanding of tone.

Portuguese: A1-A2

I have been learning Portuguese for a few months now. It's a beautiful language with a rich literary tradition. I am focusing on the phonetics and basic conversational phrases. I wanted to start Portuguese because of a group I like.
